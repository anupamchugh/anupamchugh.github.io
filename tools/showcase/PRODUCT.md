# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Delegated: the owner said "a pdf or app or web app", so plain static HTML, CSS and JS in `public/showcase/`, generated by `tools/showcase/build.py`. It drops onto GitHub Pages, the blog's `public/` folder or any host, opens from a file, and prints to PDF.

## Users
Prospective clients (founders and product teams) deciding whether to hire one independent builder for iOS, macOS and visionOS apps with on-device AI. They arrive from a blog post or a shared link, often on a phone first. Their job: judge range, craft and whether the work actually ships.

## Product Purpose
A client-facing showcase of the owner's apps: an "app of apps". Live App Store apps lead; unreleased work sits on a labelled "In the studio" shelf. Success: a visitor believes the work ships and presses "Work with me".

## Positioning
Evidence a neighbouring portfolio could not copy: real App Store links for each live app, real Xcode preview renders of screens before they ship, and real repository history (first commit and commit count) behind each app.

## Operating Context
- The blog (https://anupamchugh.github.io) already lists 18 apps in `content/apps.json`, all marked live, with App Store links, icons, platforms, tags and versions.
- Real screenshots exist in the owner's project folders for about 19 projects.
- Xcode 27.1 RenderPreview renders were taken on 24 Oct 2026 for Orbits, Glint, Quorion and Feedloom.
- Interactive prototype pages and origin data (first commit, commit count) exist per app.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Name on the page: **Anupam Chugh**.
- "Work with me" opens a form that builds a mailto: draft in the visitor's own mail app (`tools/showcase/contact-modal.html`). Nothing is sent by the page and no request leaves it. The address anupamchugh@gmail.com is shown in the form.
- Unreleased work (Glint, Maskmen and Night of the Ninja, Xthlete, Threadmark) appears only on a labelled "In the studio" shelf with no store claims. The one exception is the Assistants section, where Glint's Ask and Quorion's host assistant appear in frosted panes labelled in development.
- Names and links come from `content/apps.json`, which follows the App Store: Quorion Table (1.3.0), Orbits: Find Common Ground (shown as Orbits), EchoHunt Music (shown as EchoHunt). The page says what the store calls an app wherever it shows a shorter name. The Quorion screens are from the 1.4 playtest build and are labelled as such.
- Glint: only public Binance prices are real data on the page. Positions, P&L and charts are Xcode previews with sample numbers, and no real account, balance or position is ever shown.
- Xthlete: say only what the repo supports (colours and artwork from the public site, sample data, no backend). Do not claim a token design system.
- Left out on purpose: Landfall (real resume), SpideyRadar and Halftone (Marvel-named), internal plans.
- Do not use the name "Blood on the Clocktower" in public copy; call the app by its own name, Quorion Table.
- Undecided: whether every app on the blog is still live on the App Store; the owner should confirm before sending.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Anupam Chugh. The blog's app descriptions are written in lowercase plain first-person; treat that as the observed voice, not a binding rule.

## Evidence on Hand
- App list, descriptions, App Store links, icons: `anupamchugh.github.io/content/apps.json`, `public/app-icons/`.
- Screenshots per project (Pinprint, DocPolish, FaceFrame, LineageMirror, flyleaf, papersky, Crossing, XthleteDemo, Feedloom, MySongs, KeyMuse, PlaceMatch, Pastport, threadmark-swift).
- Preview renders and 20 prototype PDFs from the share kit.
- Absent, and must not be invented: testimonials, client names, download or revenue numbers, ratings, press.

## Product Principles
1. Show the running thing first: a real screen beats a description.
2. Every claim has a link or a number behind it; unreleased work is labelled as unreleased.
3. One person's craft, not an agency's: plain first-person voice, no corporate filler.
4. Works as a link, a phone page and a printed PDF.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast. No flashing, blinking or rapid motion anywhere; honour prefers-reduced-motion and keep content visible without JavaScript animation.
