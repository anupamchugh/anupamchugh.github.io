# Surface brief: showcase site

Scope: one static page, `public/showcase/index.html`. Visitor mode: Experience (a portfolio; the work leads from the first viewport).
Audience and job: prospective clients, usually arriving on a phone from a blog link, deciding whether this one builder ships real apps. Action: "Work with me" (blog About page). Proof on hand: 18 App Store links, real screenshots, Xcode preview renders, first-commit dates and commit counts. Constraints: no flashing or rapid motion; no invented testimonials, clients or numbers; unreleased work labelled in development; no email on the page.
Unresolved: confirm every blog-listed app is still live before sending.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one steel-framed glass partition wall. Every app is a pane; the wall is read like a map. It refuses the portfolio template (hero, row of equal icon cards, logo strip).

OWN-WORLD: Matte black glazing bars at true thickness are the only line ink. Ground is clear, seeded-white glass tinted faintly cool. Saturated colour is reserved for lit panes: each live app's own icon colour, plus cobalt, amber and oxblood for the headline, action and notes. One grotesque (Archivo, variable width and weight) set inside a pane and never across a bar; hierarchy comes from pane size and glass colour, not weight. Unreleased work is frosted glass. Hover backlights a pane; focus draws a bar-thick ring.

STORY: A visitor sees at once that this is one person who ships iOS, visionOS and on-device AI apps, believes it because real screens and App Store links sit behind the glass, and presses "Work with me" or an app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a 12-column wall of panes. The widest pane (left, about 7 columns) holds the headline "I build iOS, visionOS and on-device AI apps that ship." with one supporting line and the cobalt "Work with me" pane. Right of it, two tall panes show real screenshots of the lead apps in thin black bezels, backlit in their colours. Beneath, a row of small lit icon panes peeks under the fold. Mobile: panes restack into one column at true bar thickness, headline pane first, a lit screenshot pane second.

FORM: craft-making-glazier-colorfield-partition (dealt challenger, won over the assigned specimen-cabinet direction, which was candidate 6 of the grounded list). Seed key 88b2dc39. Raises: from the darkroom, one slow 'development' of the lit panes on load (a single eased brighten, static under reduced motion); from the specimen cabinet, a plate on each case pane naming when it was started and its platform.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
