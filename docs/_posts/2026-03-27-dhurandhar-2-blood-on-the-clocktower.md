*Spoilers ahead for Dhurandhar and Dhurandhar: The Revenge.*

In [Blood on the Clocktower](https://bloodontheclocktower.com/), the [Vigormortis](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Vigormortis) works through its minions. When they die, everyone near them gets poisoned. The Mafia gets stronger when its own people fall.

Hollywood figured this out decades ago. Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy runs on conference tables and tiny inconsistencies. The Departed is two moles performing loyalty to the wrong side. The gun is just what happens when the lie fails.

Bollywood kept choosing the gunfight. Tiger, Pathaan, War 2 — espionage like an action movie with a passport. Romeo Akbar Walter was the grounded exception — a 1971 story told straight.

Dhurandhar 2 takes the other path: the entertaining parts of social deception. Hamza is the Vigormortis. When his handler Aalam is about to expose the cover, Hamza shoots his own man. The minion keeps vouching from the grave.

Then Rakesh Bedi. Jameel Jamali — the comic relief you stopped watching — is an Indian operative fifty years deep. That's the [Vortox](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Vortox). Once you know, you replay every scene he was in.

229 minutes and what stays is the quiet deception. Aditya Dhar directed both films. His name was in the title the whole time. In every game of Mafia, the best player is the one nobody suspects is playing.
