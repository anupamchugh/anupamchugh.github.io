What happens when an outfit and a tattoo become part of the same look?

This short slideshow uses screenshots prepared for Wearover's App Store listing. It shows a before-and-after outfit, the tattoo placement controls, and a saved look with a rust shirt and a lion on the forearm.

[Play the Wearover outfit and tattoo preview](/wearover-preview/model-tattoo.mp4)

These are still screenshots, arranged into a video—not a recording of someone using the app. They were prepared for version 3.0.6, which was waiting for App Review when this preview was made. The preview does not establish that every pictured feature is available in the current App Store version.

The source photo is [Man Wearing White Shirt Standing Beside the Blue Chair](https://www.pexels.com/photo/man-wearing-white-shirt-standing-beside-the-blue-chair-7597494/) by Alexandru Molnar on Pexels. The look shown is an edited example, not an endorsement by the model.
