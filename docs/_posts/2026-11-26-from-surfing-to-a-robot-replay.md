A surf pop-up takes a moment. Trying to understand it afterwards can take much longer.

I started Breakline for my own surfing while recovering from a broken back. The idea was to look at body position in a photo or clip, frame by frame, and make a movement easier to study. From there it grew beyond surfing: gym sets, other sports, and an exploration of dance.

The part I keep coming back to is the robot. Breakline's development work takes a pose and replays the movement on a small figure. A drawing of the body and the robot sit beside each other: first the measurements, then another way to see what happened.

[See the Breakline captures and meal walkthrough](/showcase/#breakline-meals). The dance and robot images are development captures from project documentation. The walkthrough is a scripted example. They don't establish which features are in the current App Store build.

## Measurements, not a score

A knee angle is a measurement. “Good form” is a judgment, and a much bigger claim. Breakline's direction is to show positions and make the limits visible rather than give a movement a grade or pretend to provide injury advice.

The useful part isn't making a robot that looks impressive in a screenshot. It's making it easier to compare a moment in a clip with what your body was doing. That means keeping the original context, showing what was measured, and being clear when a pose couldn't be read.

## The gym has a kitchen too

The Gym work led to a different question: what happens around the workout? A trainer's plan might include meals as well as sets. Breakline's development code explores a meal plan, a plate photo, and an assistant that helps with a shopping list. There was also an experimental Swiggy Instamart handoff; the showcase explains what was and wasn't verified.

These are connected ideas, but they need their own proof. A robot replay on a drawn sample doesn't prove meal-photo recognition, just as a shopping-list demonstration doesn't validate a surfing measurement. I like the breadth of the idea. I also want each part to earn its place through real use.
