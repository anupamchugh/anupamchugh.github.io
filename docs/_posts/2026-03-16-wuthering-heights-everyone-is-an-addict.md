Watched Emerald Fennell's Wuthering Heights (2026). The standard read is doomed romance. But what struck me is simpler: every character is an addict, just to different things.

Hindley — gambling and alcohol. The slow, invisible ruin. Heathcliff — addicted to Catherine, then to revenge when he can't have her. The obsession never changes object, it just changes shape. Catherine — addicted to wanting. She needs both Heathcliff's wildness and Edgar's comfort. When she loses control of the arrangement, she breaks. Isabella — addicted to the idea of Heathcliff, chasing a version of him that never existed.

The father numbs. The hero fixates. The heroine grasps. It's not a love story. It's a study in what happens when nobody can stop.
