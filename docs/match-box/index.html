Match Box

Context for dating conversations. Kept on your Mac.

Match Box turns the screen you choose in iPhone Mirroring into reviewable local context. It keeps visible prompts, chats, and uncertainty together while you keep control of every action.

What it can understand

Visible profile prompts, interests, likes context, chat lists, and conversation bubbles. Local review points to saved facts and leaves unknowns explicit.

What it will not do

It never sends a message, likes, swipes, matches, logs in, or operates a dating service on your behalf.
