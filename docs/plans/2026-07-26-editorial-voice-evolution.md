# editorial voice evolution

## goal

Make the public archive sound increasingly like Anupam now—not like an AI rewrite of Anupam then.

## editorial contract

- The author supplies the memory, claim, scene, and final wording.
- An editor may suggest cuts, order, rhythm, transitions, and clearer nouns/verbs.
- No new personal facts, opinions, scenes, or conclusions are invented.
- A new pattern is marked as evolution before it is treated as a regression.
- Nothing moves from draft to published without explicit approval.

## states

```text
inventory → voice check → author rewrite → editorial pass → fact/link check → approved → publish
```

Use one review card per post:


## voice checks

Check for descending rhythm, short punch sentences, em-dash pivots, concrete physical verbs,
quiet endings, and openings that begin with a fact, fragment, or tension. Flag “a lot of,”
paragraph-opening “While,” semicolons, announced transitions, and generic conclusions—but do
not mechanically replace them.

## review prompt

> Read this as an editor, not a ghostwriter. Identify the thesis, strongest weave, weakest weave,
> redundant passages, missing evidence, and one possible rewrite. Preserve my facts and cadence.
> Treat unfamiliar phrasing as possible evolution. Ask before changing the idea or publishing.
