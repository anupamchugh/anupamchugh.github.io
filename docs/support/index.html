Playlist Hunter Support

Need help with the app? Here are answers to common questions.

How do I generate a playlist?

Open the AI tab, type a mood or description (like "rainy Sunday morning"), and tap Generate Playlist. The AI will find 20 matching songs from Apple Music.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

You need an Apple Music subscription for full playback. You can generate playlists and browse songs without one, but playback will be limited to previews.

What is BYOK mode?

Bring Your Own Key lets you use your own Anthropic (Claude) API key for unlimited playlist generations without purchasing credits. Go to Settings > API Key to set it up.

How do credits work?

Each AI playlist generation uses 1 credit. You get 1 free generation to try the app. Sign in with Apple and purchase credit packs for more generations. Credits never expire.

My purchase didn't go through

Go to Settings > Restore Purchases to sync your credits. If the issue persists, check your Apple ID payment settings and try again.
