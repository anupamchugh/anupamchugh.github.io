Two inks and one day

The almanac prints in black and one festival ink. Glint keeps that discipline: black for facts, indigo for the trading day. The almanac's red becomes indigo, because in Glint red only ever means a loss. Every other colour has one job and never borrows another's.

iPhone: market screens, one strip

Native tab bar, large titles, system sheets. The Day Strip sits under the title on every market screen, so you always know where in the session an answer was taken. Mobbin references: Origin (benchmark line), Fidelity and Public (price header and ranges), Yahoo (last refresh), Stake and Copilot (rows with pills), Sumeria (stat rows), Revolut (single decision sheet).

iPhone: chat, market and trade

Chat is how most questions start, so its answers are the same parts as every other screen: rows, charts and proposals, each with its receipt. The order ticket follows Kite's fields and ends at Face ID, on iPhone only. Live submission stays locked until gs-ac2b.18.

Mac: sidebar, work, receipts

A native split view. The sidebar holds the same five areas plus Alerts. The work column holds the strip, chart and answer. The ruled right margin holds the receipts: source, snapshot, completeness and gaps for whatever is selected.

TradeShot web: the same almanac, read-only

TradeShot (tradeshot.co) is Glint's website. It uses the same tokens through tokens.css, with the strip running full width under the nav. It replaces the current navy and mint look, where losses showed in yellow. Orders never appear on the web; "Review on iPhone" sends the proposal to the phone.

Scan builder: Chartink rules, receipts attached

Rules read like a sentence and compile to Glint's typed screen language. The same scan can be typed as a question in Chat. Each result row says which rule it passed, and the scan reports how many symbols it could evaluate.

Chart workspace: TradingView depth, almanac calm

On Mac and the web: a drawing-tool rail, indicator legend, four-chart layouts, compare, alerts on the chart and bar replay. The Day Strip shares the chart's time axis, so session marks and events line up with the bars.

Orbit keeps its own world

Orbit has its own token file (orbit-theme.json) and dark violet identity, so the almanac does not apply. Its rehaul is one journey: ask, nearby options, approve, saved to History. The current Discovery screen opens on a paragraph about Foundation Models; these screens open on the question and the map. You asked for Orbit alongside Glint. The Apple Maps, Airbnb and Google Maps references still need a Mobbin pull from a session with the connector.
