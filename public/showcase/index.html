Assistants that run on the phone

Three of the apps have one. Each can only use the few tools it is given, and the limits are part of the screen.

Orbits

The planner has four tools: your saved places, Apple Maps search, routes and weather. Each has a switch. Turn weather off and Orbits says it cannot answer instead of guessing.

Build in development, 22 October 2026 · fixture model standing in for the on-device one

Quorion Table

The host's assistant can call exactly three tools, and nothing else. The model proposes and the rules decide: a suggestion that names a dead seat or an unknown one is thrown out and the plain rule is used instead.

In development · covered by unit tests, no screens of it yet

  1. Role counts Checks that the mix of characters fits the number of players.
  2. Night order Works out who wakes, and when, for the first night or any later one.
  3. Bluff sets Builds three sets of three characters to bluff with, and refuses when fewer than three qualify.

Glint

Ask Glint calls a tool before it answers a question about a move. Ask why BTCUSDT is moving and it reads the last day of price and volume, says what it found, and lists where each number came from under the answer. It has no order tools: the first thing it says is that it never places orders.

In development · the answer is from a build that is not merged yet, captured 25 October 2026 on the iPhone Duo simulator, signed out, on the on-device model and live public Binance prices

Glint

A native Swift market desk with live watchlists, sector heatmaps, a portfolio view, a chart sheet and a Live Activity that frames the trading day like a cricket innings. Orders stay locked. The three phones and the Lock Screen card below are Xcode previews with sample numbers.

First commit 6 Aug 2026 · 4,045 commits · in development

The chart sheet and native candlesGlint · five screens · in development

The chart sheet

Latest main · rendered and captured 24 October 2026 · in development

Open a name in Glint and its chart starts as one price line. Pick 1M, 3M, 1Y or All, and the line is green or red by how the period went. Touch and drag along it to read the price and the time under your finger. Candles stay one tap away. Three of the four screens are Xcode previews with a fixed sample walk, and the picture says so. The fourth is the running app on live public Binance prices.

Native candles

The candle chart, drawn natively instead of in a web view: candles, two moving averages, volume and a stop level, over the same chart frame as the price line. It is the next step after the price line.

An Xcode preview with sample data, 24 October 2026. The work is on a branch that is not merged yet, so it is in no build.

What is real here

Live
Prices for BTC, ETH and gold come straight from Binance's public stream.
Sample
Positions, profit and loss, the NIFTY chart, the sector heatmap and the Live Activity are Xcode previews with made-up numbers.
Paper only
Trade tickets are simulated. Nothing in the app sends an order.
Never shown
Any real account, balance or position.
