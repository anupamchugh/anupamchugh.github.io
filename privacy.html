Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2026

Find Common Ground is built by Anupam Chugh as a free app. This page informs you of our policies regarding your data.

Data Collection

Find Common Ground does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays on your device using Apple SwiftData.

Location Data

The App uses your location to show nearby places, detect visits (optional), and send local notifications near saved places (optional). Location data is processed entirely on-device and never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple MapKit for maps. When you paste a Google Maps URL, the App fetches place data from a lightweight proxy API. Only the URL is sent; no personal information is included.

Data Sharing

Find Common Ground does not share your data with any third parties. Match comparisons happen locally on-device.

Analytics

The App does not include any analytics SDKs or tracking.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
